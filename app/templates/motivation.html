A Concern of Semicolons

Why LabGemma?

AI Assisted Programming Lab and Personalized Learning System

The problem

University programming labs still depend on a lot of manual work and strict pass or fail judging. This is slow for teachers and unfair to many students.

  • Teachers spend many hours making problems, writing test cases, and checking every submission by hand.
  • Normal online judges only look at the final output. A student who gets CE, WA, or RE often gets zero, even when the code shows that they understood most of the problem.
  • Using only AI to grade is not safe. Without running the code or following a proper rubric, it can give marks that are not fair or consistent.
  • After the marks are given, students usually do not get practice based on their own mistakes. So their weak areas stay weak and they improve very slowly.

Our insight

Inspired by the ICER 2025 paper Rubric Is All You Need (Pathak et al.), question specific rubrics give fairer logical assessment than generic or blind LLM scoring.

LabGemma uses rubric specific evaluation with Gemma 4 and teacher approved rubrics. AI is used only where it adds real value.

This cuts the teacher's workload a lot. Gemma drafts the problems, test cases, and rubrics, and helps grade the hard cases, so teachers spend their time reviewing instead of doing everything by hand.

How LabGemma works

1 Generate

The instructor provides a prompt, and Gemma 4 generates the programming problem, hidden test cases, and a question specific grading rubric.

2 Approve

The instructor reviews and approves everything before publishing the lab.

3 Solve

Students solve the problem inside LabGemma using C, C++, Python, or Java.

4 Judge first

Every submission is executed against hidden test cases.

  • Accepted (AC): Full marks are awarded instantly.
  • CE / WA / RE / Partial Pass: The submission moves to AI evaluation.

5 Rubric specific AI evaluation

Gemma 4 evaluates failed or partial submissions using the teacher approved rubric and performs a consistency check before suggesting marks.

6 Teacher in control

Teachers can review, edit, or override every AI generated score. The instructor always makes the final decision.

7 Personalized learning

After each lab, LabGemma analyzes the student's performance and automatically generates personalized practice problems based on their weak areas, creating a continuous learning loop instead of just giving marks.
